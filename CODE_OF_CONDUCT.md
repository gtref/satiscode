# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to making participation in Satiscode respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- Using considerate and inclusive language
- Giving and receiving technical feedback professionally
- Focusing on the work and its impact
- Respecting different backgrounds and experience levels

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, and deliberate disruption.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential where possible. Maintainers may remove comments, restrict participation, or take other action needed to keep the project constructive.
