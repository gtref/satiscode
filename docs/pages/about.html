About Project

The original idea for Satiscode came from VS Code. The motivation behind it was frustration with IDEs constantly connecting to the internet, fetching manifests, and cluttering storage with background extensions and telemetry.

Satiscode is 100% Anti-AI and focused on offline productivity. The installation size is standard and self-contained (between 600 MB and 1 GB). You get a complete IDE that works seamlessly without requiring Wi-Fi, external cloud dependencies, or AI integrations.
