About The Project

The original idea for Satiscode was inspired by VS Code. The project stemmed from a frustration with modern IDEs constantly connecting to the internet, fetching manifests, and cluttering storage with background extensions and telemetry.

Satiscode is 100% Anti-AI and focused entirely on offline productivity. The installation size is standard and self-contained (between 600 MB and 1 GB). You get a complete IDE that works seamlessly without requiring Wi-Fi, external cloud dependencies, or AI integrations.
