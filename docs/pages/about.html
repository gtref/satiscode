SATISCODE

About Project

The original idea for satiscode came from VS Code. The reason for this idea was that I was sick of the ide constantly connecting to the internet and fetching manifests and extensions that take up all of my storage. And the AI, Oh that bloody AI. How I wish they did not add that feature. But no more do I have to yell at AI to fix my bugs and stuff because this IDE is 100% Anti AI. And because of this, satiscode IDE is a single 600 MB to 1GB install. Though this may seem like a lot. You are essentially downloading an IDE that works with no wifi, no externel librarys and NO AI!!
